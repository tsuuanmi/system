A heavy change's frontier review finds its Worker, and a Worker that proves a step but is not free now makes it wait, bounded, never a counted config error (T282):
- What was found: routing already serves a three-tier Worker at frontier and keeps a busy or out-of-quota Worker as one that proves the work. The one path by which a Worker that proves a step was read as one that does not: an agent Worker whose runtime probe was late (the last past `probeTtlMs` and the next not finished, or one timed out) listed no capabilities, so its step failed at once as `config_error` ("no Worker proves review, local-data"), not retried, its attempt spent. The live T271 failure's reason was cut, so this is its likely cause, not a confirmed one.
- Now such a Worker still lists what its runtime's last available probe proved (`validate`, `persistent-conversation`, `dossier` included; its role's offers when its first probe is the late one), reads `unavailable`, and says when its next probe's result is due (`capability-descriptor@1.12` adds `expectedBack`). A probe that failed or proved less, a signed-out account, or a pool that cannot be read still proves nothing, as before.
- A refusal whose Worker proves the step and is out of quota, at a limit with no assigned account's reset to wait for, or late, now carries `AdmissionFailure.retryAt` (when it is worth routing again), and the workflow engine waits for it: the node stays `READY`, sends nothing, spends no attempt, and is routed again then (a `recovery` decision `wait`, rule `workflow.recovery.retry`), bounded with the limit's wait by `maxLimitWaitHours` from its first wait; past it, it fails with the refusal's kind, never `config_error`. Only no configured Worker proving the step at any tier is a configuration problem. A Worker unavailable with nothing to say when (signed out, a failed probe, a challenge) still fails the step at once for the operator. A node waiting for a pooled account's limit, which used to fail at once as `quota_exhausted`, now waits within the bound.
- A failure's message lists the refusals of the Workers that prove the work first, the nearest to taking it (the fewest failed checks) before the others, at most 700 characters (a longer line clipped), then how many more the decision lists; `rejections` and the `admission` decision keep every Worker.
- Right after a host starts, a Worker source whose first describe is still waiting for its runtimes' first probes (past the router's 5 s) is no longer read as having no Worker that proves the step (the live "no Worker proves develop, local-data, dossier" at a restart): the failure says the source has not described its Workers yet since it started and carries a `retryAt` 30 s on, so the step waits within the same bound. A refusal that carries `retryAt` is never `config_error` (`admissionKind`). A source silent after one of its describes has landed is as before.
