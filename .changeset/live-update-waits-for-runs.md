A live update waits for running agent runs before it stops the unit (T234). On 2026-10-26 the update to c2a45415 stopped `system.service` while T223's develop step ran: the unit's `ExecStop` drain reached `TimeoutStopSec` and the daemon exited, so the run failed `reconciliation_failed` and its usage was never counted. Now `scripts/live-update.sh --apply`, after its capture and before the stop, pauses dispatch (unless the operator paused it or it is off; the update's own pause is lifted once the profile runs again, after the update or its rollback, or at the bound) and waits until no workflow step runs. It reads every `RUNNING` step of every running run from the store (read-only, on a Worker or not, with no bound) and what the roster adds (every `--runs-every` seconds, 30 by default), up to `--runs-wait` minutes (90 by default), and says what it waits for: each run, its step, its Worker, and for how long. The roster's bounds (the newest 500 runs, the 256 Workers listed) are reported, never read as nothing running, and a store that cannot be read counts as running. At the bound it stops with exit 1 and leaves the unit running, unless `--cut-running` is given: then it stops the unit as before and names the runs it cuts. A dry run prints what `--apply` would wait for. The drain's own deadline does not change. `scripts/live-update-check.ts` gains `running`, `await-idle`, `dispatch-pause`, and `dispatch-resume`.
