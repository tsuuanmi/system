The live update holds the Workers to what they were before it (T219, operator D82 #4: on 2026-10-26 the update to 803ac4b8 passed every upgrade check while all four Codex Workers were unavailable for about 40 minutes, their probe failing on the host's bubblewrap 0.4.0). `scripts/live-update-check.ts capture` reads each Worker's availability from the roster (MCP `roster`, as `system roster --json`) into `before.json`; `verify` reads it again every 10 s, up to `--workers-wait` seconds (600 by default, two probe TTLs), until every Worker that took work before the update (`ready` or `busy`) takes work again. One that stays `unavailable` or `quarantined`, or is no longer listed, fails the check with the roster's reason (its account's, else its source's `unavailableReason`), so `scripts/live-update.sh` rolls back as on any failed check; one resting at a usage limit is a note. Both print the Workers before and after, one line each. `live-update.sh` takes `--workers-wait <seconds>` and passes it on.
