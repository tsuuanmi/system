Live updates at any time: a cut is cheap, and everything it cuts resumes by itself (T269; the operator, 2026-10-26: "stop instead of waiting for a quiet moment, since we can resume"). On the update to 2a64dad8 with `--runs-wait 3 --cut-running` the cut runs came back as free retries on their sessions, but the unit's stop still took ten minutes (its ExecStop's `system shutdown --deadline 600` drained the cut steps anyway), and a result whose `tests` check the stop cut was held for the operator (`tests: not-run`).
- A check the host cut runs again. The `agents` source marks each check it starts with the daemon that started it; one an earlier daemon left started (it stopped or crashed meanwhile), or killed before its end by its stop, came to nothing, so at the next ask, after the restart, it runs again on the same result tree instead of answering `error`. The re-run is a `gate` decision of rule `agent-workers.check-cut-rerun` ("cut by the host, re-run", with the cuts so far), among the `check-run@1.0` evidence's inputs, and an `agent-workers.check.rerun` event. The engine asks for the checks again after a restart already, so the result is accepted, or sent back, on what the check came to, never held for the operator because it was cut. Bounded: a check cut three times on one subject stays an `error`. A check that ended keeps its outcome (INV-4), and one a stop of its run killed in the same daemon (the operator's stop, a lost lease) stays the `error` it was stored as.
- `scripts/live-update.sh` holds steps, waits for the steps in flight up to `--runs-wait` (now 5 minutes, was 90), then cuts by default (`--no-cut` stops at the bound as before). When it may cut, it stops the daemon itself with the CLI's `shutdownHost` and a short drain, `--cut-deadline` (default 60 s, at most 600): agent runs are cut at once, their sessions and worktrees kept (a free retry, ADR-0069), a website turn already sent is reconciled at the next start and never resent; the unit's stop then finds the daemon gone. A cut never falls back to the unit's drain: a CLI without `shutdownHost`, or a short stop that fails with the daemon still running, refuses the update with nothing stopped (steps released, dispatch resumed); `--no-cut` waits instead. The steps it cut are written to `<attempt>/cut.json`, and after the upgrade checks the update reports how each resumed (free retry, resumed in its session, not settled yet, or counted) and each cut check that ran again (`cut-report.txt`). `scripts/live-update-check.ts` gains `stop-cut` and `cut-report`, and `await-idle --cut-file`.

See the runbook "Update the live install", docs/design/workflow-engine.md "A run's required checks", and docs/design/agent-runtimes.md "A run's checks".
