Help for an operator who is not a developer (T126):
- A **Help** page in the navigation explains how System works in plain words, with links into the real pages: who does the work, how a task goes from idea to merged, what waits for you and why, what runs without asking, and what to do when something is stuck.
- A glossary gives each word the pages use (Worker, quota, resting, attempt, review, merge, policy, quarantined, stalled, …) in one plain sentence, each with its own anchor.
- Wherever a page shows such a word, a **?** button beside it opens its sentence in place, by click, tap, or keyboard (never hover alone), with a link to the entry in Help.
- Every empty state says what would appear there and how it gets there.
