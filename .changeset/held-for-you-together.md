`/ui` shows the results held for the operator together, in a "Held for you" section above the reviews on Home: oldest expiry first, each row with its task, why it is held, its checks, each reviewer's verdict and provider family, the first lines of its digest, and its change size. Select rows and accept or send them back together: each is still its own decision on the exact result shown, recorded one by one with the same note; a row with a check not run asks for why in place and is not sent without it; a refused row shows the server's reason and stays; a decided row and its card leave at once, and Home's counts follow.
