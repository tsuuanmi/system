A Worker's deferred tool call no longer stalls a board task's attempt (T199): attempts were held `BLOCKED` for one to two hours on deferred read-only commands (`git status --short`, `ls`, `sleep 1`), and a rejection or an expiry ended the attempt.
- A rejected, expired, or withdrawn call is denied, never run, and the step's session continues once, as the approved path does: its brief says the call was denied and why, the gate denies that exact call in the session (the agent's tool result is the denial), and the Worker goes on without it. Recorded as a `recovery` decision `deny` (rule `workflow.deferred-call`) and a `workflow.run.continued` event with `denied: true`. A call unanswered past the step's deadline is denied the same way even before the queue's sweep records its expiry, and an approval given after that deadline (the engine down across it) is denied too (cause `late`). The attempt ends for the operator only when the policy now forbids the call's class or when the session cannot resume.
- A denied call stays denied in its session: before allowing any call, `auto` included, the gate checks the request it last opened for that exact call, and the engine's denial of it. The engine keeps that denial on the gate's own row of the request (new `agent-workers` migration 18: `agent_tool_requests.denied_at`, `denial_decision`, `denial_cause`; written through `ctx.agentWorkers.denyToolCall` in the transaction of the `deny` decision, and with nothing to keep it the attempt ends instead), and the gate reads it by session, never from the event log. So a policy change that lists the command, a late approval, a later attempt or chained task that continues the session in another workflow run, or the event log's retention never lets it run, across restarts too.
- New workflow setting `deferredCommandMinutes` (default 10; 0 waits until the request expires, as before): a `command` call unanswered that long after it was asked is denied for that call alone; its request is withdrawn, and the denial (cause `bound`) is the call's expiry. The bound is on the step's wait decision, so a restart keeps it.
- A Worker whose sandbox writes its workspace is told, after its prompt, the calls its gate lets run unasked under the autonomy policy as it stands: reading tools, the read-only inspection commands (absolute paths), writing tools, and the studio policy's commands list, its exact lines.
- The gate's denial of a call whose request was withdrawn now says it was withdrawn before a verdict, and every such denial tells the agent to go on without the call.
